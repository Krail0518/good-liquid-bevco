Why launches stall

Most new drinks stall in the same three places.

01
The formula

Kitchen recipes separate, fade, or spoil once they meet real production. We turn your idea into a formula that holds up at scale.

02
The label

Nutrition panels, ingredient order, and claims all carry rules. A label mistake can mean reprinting every can.

03
The first run

Large copackers want tens of thousands of cans before you have a single customer. Our minimum is 200 cases per flavor, about 4,800 cans, enough to test a real market.

What we do not make

Check this before you buy. We are not the right fit for:

  • PET or plastic bottles (we fill aluminum cans and 750 ml glass)
  • Cold brew coffee (we make coffee drinks from concentrate)
  • Kosher or organic certified products
  • Drinks without a preservative (water is the exception)
  • Four pack paperboard cartons
  • Tunnel pasteurized products (we use flash pasteurization)

FAQ

Questions

Do I own my formula?

Your formula is exclusive to your brand, with no license fee as long as Good Liquid makes it. You can also buy the formula outright for $15,000 per SKU, or license it to another manufacturer for $6,000 per SKU per year.

What is the smallest run you can do?

Our minimum is 200 cases per flavor, which is 4,800 cans. The 50L pilot batch in both launch packages is for validation only. Your run cost depends on volume, can size, and packaging.

I already have a recipe. Do I still need formulation?

Usually yes. Home recipes almost always need adjustments for stability, shelf life, and scale. We start from what you have.

How long does a launch take?

Plan on 8 to 12 weeks: 2 to 4 weeks of formulation, 2 to 4 weeks of material sourcing, and about 4 weeks to your production slot once everything is in house.
